# Sinta Reference

Reference notes on Sinta ranking, ARJUNA accreditation, and the anatomy and
technical requirements of a Sinta-targeted journal article.

## Sinta vs ARJUNA

- **Sinta** — [sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id](https://sinta.kemdiktisaintek.go.id) — is a
  **ranking portal**. It publishes the rank (S1–S6) assigned to a journal. Sinta does
  **not** issue templates and does not perform accreditation itself.
- **ARJUNA** — [arjuna.kemdiktisaintek.go.id](https://arjuna.kemdiktisaintek.go.id) —
  is the body that **performs accreditation** of journals and feeds the results into Sinta.
- Sinta does **not** issue templates. Always obtain the article template from the target
  journal's own OJS site (see *How to locate a journal's Author Guidelines* below).

## Rank table

| Level | Score band | Angka Kredit | Language | Typical target |
|-------|------------|--------------|----------|----------------|
| S1 | 85–100 | 25 AK | predominantly English | International-facing, high-visibility journal |
| S2 | 70–84 | 25 AK | predominantly English | Strong national/international journal |
| S3 | 60–69 | 15 AK | accepts Indonesian | Reputable national journal |
| S4 | 50–59 | 15 AK | Indonesian | National journal |
| S5 | 40–49 | 10 AK | Indonesian | Local/national journal |
| S6 | 30–39 | 10 AK | Indonesian | Emerging local journal |

Sources: Permenristekdikti 9/2018 and Kepmendiktisaintek 63/M/Kep/2025.

> **Language note:** articles in **S3–S6** are commonly written in Indonesian —
> see [docs/bahasa-artikel.md](bahasa-artikel.md) for the register rules and
> [docs/sinta-corpus/MANIFEST.md](sinta-corpus/MANIFEST.md) for the verified
> journal corpus.

## 2026 rule change

- Since **Permendiktisaintek 9/2026**, new accreditation yields only ranks **1–4**.
- Existing **S5/S6** journals remain valid until their accreditation expires.
- Accreditation lasts **5 years**.

## ARJUNA 8 indicators + weights

| # | Indicator | Weight |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Penamaan | 3 |
| 2 | Kelembagaan | 4 |
| 3 | Penyuntingan | 17 |
| 4 | **Substansi Artikel** | **39** |
| 5 | Gaya Penulisan | 12 |
| 6 | Penampilan | 8 |
| 7 | Keberkalaan | 6 |
| 8 | Penyebarluasan | 11 |

> **Highlight:** *Substansi Artikel* carries the largest weight (**39**). Content quality
> is the dominant factor in accreditation, so effort should be concentrated on the
> substance of the article above cosmetic concerns.

## Article anatomy

- **Title** — concise, ≤12–16 words.
- **Authors** — names without academic titles, followed by affiliation and ORCID.
- **Abstract** — 150–250 words, in both Indonesian (ID) and English (EN).
- **Keywords** — 3–6 keywords.
- **Body** — IMRaD sections (Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion).
- **References** — complete, consistent, and cited in text.

## Technical spec table

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Paper | A4 |
| Margins | ~2.5–3 cm |
| Font | Times New Roman (or Arial/Cambria), 10–12 pt |
| Spacing | 1.0 or 1.15 |
| Columns | 1–2 columns |
| Length | 3,000–8,000 words / 10–20 pages |
| Submission files | `.docx` + `.pdf` |

## Citation styles by discipline

- **APA** — social sciences and education.
- **IEEE** — engineering and computer science.
- **Vancouver** — health sciences.

## Mandatory tooling

- **Reference manager** — Mendeley or Zotero.
- **Similarity check** — Turnitin, similarity ≤ 20–25%.
- **Language check** — Grammarly.

## How to locate a journal's Author Guidelines

1. Open the target journal's OJS site.
2. Navigate to **Author Guidelines** (and **Focus & Scope**).
3. Download the official template and follow it exactly.

## Pre-submit checklist

- [ ] Write inside the target journal's own template.
- [ ] Include a bilingual abstract (ID + EN).
- [ ] Use a reference manager for all citations.
- [ ] Number and title all figures and tables.
- [ ] Prepare a separate blind-review file (author identity removed).
- [ ] Attach the ethics/originality letter.
